# Canonical Sector Examples

## Definition

Inputs:
- What is venture capital?
- Define venture capital.
- What does venture capital mean?

Sector:
`definition | venture_capital`

Canonical mode:
3-sentence definition.

## Research

Inputs:
- Find information about Irakli Kashibadze.
- Who is Irakli Kashibadze?
- Research Irakli Kashibadze.

Sector:
`entity_public_information_lookup | irakli_kashibadze`

Canonical structure:
1. Identity
2. Current role
3. Previous roles
4. Major projects / contributions
5. Education / affiliations
6. Source caveat only if necessary

Peripheral facts found in only one retrieval pass do not automatically expand the answer.
